Writing Jail is a focused Markdown editor for macOS, designed for the moments when the next sentence needs your full attention.

For people who love to write, or simply have to write, Writing Jail removes every distraction and creates a place to focus entirely on your words.

Choose an existing Markdown file or create a new one, start a timed writing session, and keep working in a clean full-screen editor. During a focus session, Writing Jail blocks app switching as fully as macOS allows, including the Dock, menu bar, Cmd-Tab, and supported system shortcuts.

WRITE IN MARKDOWN

Open and edit local .md files directly. Automatic saves run every 30 seconds, with additional saves when a session starts or ends.

CHOOSE YOUR FOCUS

Select a duration from 5 minutes to 24 hours. Sessions of 30 minutes or longer require confirmation.

DARK AND IVORY

Switch between a low-light dark workspace and a warm ivory page. Text size can be adjusted with Cmd-Plus and Cmd-Minus.

MULTIPLE DISPLAYS

Mirror the editor across displays, show the system wallpaper, choose a custom image, or fill every secondary display with a solid color.

YOUR FILES STAY LOCAL

Writing Jail does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It accesses only files and folders you explicitly select. No account, analytics, advertising, or cloud synchronization is required.

Input Blocking is optional. With Accessibility permission, Writing Jail can more reliably block focus-breaking shortcuts. Without that permission, Limited Mode still provides an in-app kiosk-style workspace. Writing Jail never records or transmits the keys you type.

macOS system controls such as forced restart and physical power remain outside the control of a regular app.
